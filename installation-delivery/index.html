Installation & delivery

We check the space and access before confirming your installation, and set out who does what in your itemised quotation.

  1. 1Check the area
  2. 2Assess the space
  3. 3Prepare the site
  4. 4Agree responsibilities
  5. 5Delivery and handover

Step 1

Check the area

We install in England and Wales. Scotland needs an individual postcode assessment. Northern Ireland, islands and other locations need separate arrangements.

We take a postcode so that we can review where you are. A postcode that looks valid does not on its own mean we can install there, and we do not run an automatic coverage check. We confirm availability with you.

Step 2

Assess the space

Photographs and approximate measurements start the review. Tell us where the sauna would go and how it would get there.

Straightforward sites may be assessed through photos or a video call. More complex sites may need a visit. Either way, we check the space and access before your order is confirmed.

Step 3

Prepare the site

A checklist of what we will ask about. We do not give generic measurements, because the right figures depend on your model, heater and site.

  • Where the sauna would stand, and whether the ground there is level and firm
  • Base dimensions, load and level, once confirmed for your model
  • Access width and height along the whole route
  • Corners, gates and steps the cabin has to pass
  • Overhead obstructions such as branches, cables or structures
  • Drainage near the planned position
  • Equipment and maintenance clearances around the cabin
  • Electrical preparation, assessed separately and following the approved equipment specification

Model-specific requirements

Base size, clearances, access requirements and weight come from the manufacturer’s drawings for your model and heater, and we confirm them with you during the site review.

Step 4

Agree responsibilities

Your itemised quotation names who does each task.

Within our installation scope

  • Assembly or positioning, as agreed
  • Fitting supplied components, within the agreed scope
  • Installation checks
  • Handover with you

Assessed separately

  • Ground preparation
  • Electrical connection
  • Specialist lifting
  • Non-standard access work

The quotation says whether each of these is ours, yours, or your contractor’s.

Step 5

Delivery and handover

We confirm the schedule and site readiness before delivery. After installation checks, we hand over with you and give you an accessible first point of contact for care questions.

Lead times are confirmed in your quotation. They depend on a confirmed specification and production scheduling, so we do not promise installation dates in advance.

The whole journey

From consultation to aftercare

  1. Consultation. We talk through your space, who will use the sauna and the model that suits.
  2. Site assessment. Photos or a video call for straightforward sites; a visit where it is more complex.
  3. Specification and quotation. An itemised quotation, with bespoke changes confirmed by the manufacturer first.
  4. Order and production. Built to agreed specifications by our European manufacturing partner.
  5. Delivery and installation. Scheduled once your site is ready, then checked.
  6. Handover and aftercare. A walk-through, and one place to go with questions.

Planning questions

Common questions

What base does a sauna need?

A sauna needs a suitable, level base. The right type and size depends on the model and on your ground, and ground preparation is assessed separately from the sauna itself.

We confirm the base requirements for your chosen model during the site review, before your order is confirmed.

Prepare the site →

Can a sauna be delivered into my garden?

Access matters as much as space. We ask about gates, paths, steps, corners and overhead obstructions, starting with photographs and approximate measurements.

Straightforward sites may be assessed from photos or a video call. More complex sites may need a visit. Specialist lifting and non-standard access work are assessed separately.

How we assess your space →

How much room do I need around it?

A cabin’s exterior size is not the whole picture. You also need room for the base, clearances around the cabin, and a route in for delivery.

We confirm clearances against the approved specification for your model and heater, rather than estimating them from the cabin’s size.

Read the planning guide →

What does the starting price include?

The starting price is for the cabin as described on each model page, including VAT.

Delivery and installation scope, and anything that depends on your site, such as ground preparation, electrical connection or specialist lifting, are set out in your itemised quotation. It is not a confirmed installed total.

What happens after I enquire?

The team reviews your project and aims to respond within one business day during working hours.

From there we help you choose a model, check your space and access, confirm the specification and quote, and arrange installation and handover once your site requirements have been checked.
