Logo, palette and type

Three directions were drawn and checked in the real header, footer, favicon and small-size contexts. Direction C is implemented.

The chosen design

The symbol is an A and an E sharing one upright: a leaning leg and a vertical stem, with a single bar that is both the A’s crossbar and the E’s middle arm. It is plain structural geometry, so it holds at favicon size, and the ember bar is the only place the accent colour appears in the mark.

The wordmark is set in Newsreader, outlined so it needs no installed font, with the typeface’s own italic ampersand. It is typeset, not hand-drawn lettering, and the ampersand is the typeface’s, not a custom character. A hand-drawn ampersand was tried and rejected because it looked clumsy next to the letters.

Three directions

  • A. Architectural wordmark

    Elegant and calm, but the cabin-frame mark reads as a generic window icon and adds little.

  • B. Timber and warmth

    Rejected. Stepped boards with an accent read as a bar chart, which is the wrong kind of product.

  • C. A / E construction

    Chosen. Distinctive at small sizes, built from simple structural geometry, and the accent has one job.
