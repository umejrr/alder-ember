# Photography brief: every image on the site

For Alder & Ember and the photographer. It lists each photograph the website needs, where each one appears, how the site crops it, and what must be in the frame. It is written from the layouts that are built, so finished files can go straight in.

**Status.** All 17 image slots, the hero and the link-preview image now show photographs supplied by the owner and approved on 22 October 2026 (dark timber cabin, glazed front, dusk, a clipped hedge and silver birch). They are the look benchmark for any new work. They are 1448 to 1884 px wide, and the photographer, licence terms and which sauna each shows are not yet recorded, so this brief remains the specification for a professional shoot or higher-resolution replacements. For the hero's own specification, see Appendix A.

## 1. The look, in one paragraph

Quiet, grounded and architectural: a considered timber cabin in a real UK garden, photographed at **dusk or in soft overcast light**, lit warmly from inside, with natural colour and nothing theatrical. Dark, fine-grained timber; a lawn, a clipped hedge, silver birch; a gravel base. Interiors are calm and honest about space and scale. Details are tactile and true to colour. No people's faces, no added steam or smoke, no heavy orange grade, no HDR look.

Palette to keep in mind (the site's own): warm canvas `#f4f0e8`, deep forest `#111c16`, timber `#795b45`, and a very small ember accent `#a54f32`.

## 2. Ground rules for every image

1. **Accuracy comes first.** A photograph labelled as the Rowan, Alder or Ember must show that model, exactly as it is sold, in its standard configuration. Never use one model standing in for another. Note the model and configuration for every file.
2. **One garden for the three exteriors**, ideally the hero's garden, so the range looks like one place and compares fairly.
3. **No people's faces.** Where people appear (installation), they are shown from behind or at a distance, with signed releases.
4. **No added steam, smoke, fire or lens effects.** No heavy colour grade: neutral white balance, true timber colour, a warm glow from the interior only.
5. **No brands, logos, competitor products, house numbers, number plates or recognisable neighbours** in frame.
6. **Verticals stay vertical.** Use a tilt-shift lens or shoot a little wide and correct in post. No tilted horizons, no fisheye.
7. **Do not invent.** Construction details come from the manufacturer. Show the approved heater package only. No measurements, labels or annotations in the frame.
8. **Shoot with margin.** The site crops the same photograph to 3:2, 4:3, 5:4, 4:5 and square in different places. Keep everything essential inside the **central 88% of the width and 85% of the height**.
9. **Real projects only** for installation images, with the customer's written permission.

## 4. Image by image

### 4.1 Model exteriors (`rowan-exterior`, `alder-exterior`, `ember-exterior`)

These are the most-seen images on the site: the large stage of the model explorer on the home page (3:2), the collection and model cards, the first image on each model page (4:3), and the Alder campaign page.

- **Same everything for all three.** One garden, one angle, one camera height, one lens, one time of day. Shoot from the **front-left at a three-quarter angle**, so the front (door or glazing) and one side are visible. **Camera height about 1.3 m**, lens **28 to 35 mm equivalent**, camera level. The cabin fills about **55% of the frame width**. Do not enlarge or shrink a model in post: bigger models should look bigger.
- **Light.** The hero's light: **15 to 40 minutes after sunset**, deep blue sky, interior lit, the lawn falling into shade. Back-up set in soft overcast daylight.
- **Setting.** Lawn in front, hedge and birch behind, gravel base. Nothing else in frame: no bins, hoses, furniture, vehicles, other buildings.
- **Door closed, interior lit.** The glazed front or windows glow; the interior timber is visible.
- **Show what is distinctive.** Rowan: the compact cabin, with room around it to read as a small-garden building. Alder: the glazed front. Ember: the larger cabin, framed so the **changing area** is visible.
- **Composition.** Cabin centred in the 20% to 80% band of the width, 8% of air all round. Quiet sky or foliage in the top 12%.

### 4.2 Model interiors (`rowan-interior`, `alder-interior`, `ember-interior`)

Shown in the model page gallery and in the page's interior section.

- **Position.** In the doorway corner, **camera at bench-top height (1.0 to 1.2 m)**, lens **16 to 24 mm equivalent**, level, no fisheye.
- **Show.** Both bench levels with their depth, the heater with its guard and stones, the wall and ceiling timber, the door or glazing, the floor. The viewer should be able to judge how many people sit comfortably.
- **Scale cues, sparingly.** One folded undyed-linen towel on the upper bench, one plain timber bucket and ladle on the lower. Nothing else.
- **Light.** The sauna's own lights on, dimmed, plus balanced daylight or dusk light through the glass. No flash glare on glass. No steam: the heater is off or warm, never "smoking".
- **Accuracy.** The standard bench layout and the approved heater package. If the heater package is not confirmed before the shoot, say so, because it may need re-shooting.

### 4.3 Model details (`rowan-detail`, `alder-detail`, `ember-detail`)

The third image in each model gallery. Each one is specific to its model.

- **The Rowan:** the electric heater and its control panel with the bench edge beside it, from a three-quarter angle. Approved heater package only.
- **The Alder:** the **glazed front seen from inside**, looking out into the garden at dusk, so the glass frames the lit lawn and the hedge. It shows the model's main feature and the idea of the site: from the garden into the warmth.
- **The Ember:** the **changing area**: bench, hooks, floor, and the door through to the sauna. Calm, tidy and honestly scaled.
- Shoot **3:2 horizontal**, subject centred with 15% margin, shallow depth of field is fine (f/4 to f/5.6).

### 4.4 The craft chapter: "Considered in every detail" (`detail-timber`, `detail-interior-finish`, `detail-construction`, `detail-glazing-heater`)

A dark chapter on the home page with three tabs (exterior, interior, construction); each picture is cropped to **5:4 on desktop** and shown larger on mobile. Shoot every close-up with margin.

- **`detail-timber`, exterior cladding.** The thermally modified timber: board joints, a corner, the grain, the fixings. **Raking low light** to show texture, true colour, fresh and unweathered, no stickers or markings. Shoot **a vertical 4:5 and a horizontal 3:2 from the same position**, with the key feature centred.
- **`detail-interior-finish`, interior and bench.** A bench edge, the smooth timber surface, the board spacing, a backrest. Warm, low, even light. No steam, no sweat.
- **`detail-construction`, insulated build-up.** **From the manufacturer only.** A real cut-away sample of the wall, or the wall photographed during the build before the cladding closes. Square-on, even neutral light, plain background. **No ruler, no dimensions and no annotation in frame**: the site must not state a thickness the manufacturer has not given. Ask the manufacturer for the layer list so the caption can be written accurately.
- **`detail-glazing-heater`, glazing and heater.** Two frames, one slot: (a) the glazing frame and door handle from outside at dusk; (b) the heater and controls from inside. **Only the approved heater package.** The site uses one and keeps the other for the gallery.

### 4.5 Process (`site-access`, `installation-process`)

Both must be **real**. The site says "Genuine installation photography only" and a stock substitute must not stand in for evidence.

- **`site-access`, a real access check** on a real visit: a gate, a path, steps, a narrow gap, with a tape measure or the team's hands in frame to show why access is checked. **Overcast daylight.** No faces, no house numbers, no vehicle plates. It appears twice: on the home page beside "From your space to first use" and on Installation & delivery.
- **`installation-process`, a real installation:** the team positioning a cabin on its prepared base. Shoot from the side, from behind, or at a distance, or with signed releases. Visible PPE. Plain, unbranded clothing and vehicles. Include the **level, compacted base**. If a qualified electrician is photographed making the connection, only with their consent.
- Capture **extras while there** (section 8): arrival and delivery, the base before the cabin, the cabin on the base, the finished garden.

### 4.6 The closing image (`closing-warmth`)

The last image on the home page, shown at about 44% of the width at 4:3: the invitation to start.

- Our recommendation: **the Alder's glazed front seen from the sauna bench at dusk, looking out to the lit garden**, the mirror image of the hero. Dim warm interior in the foreground, the blue garden in the glass.
- Alternative: a quiet bench interior in warm low light. No people, no towels piled up, no steam.

### 4.7 The social-share image (`social-share`)

The picture shown when the site is shared on messages and social platforms. The site shows no link preview until an approved image of Alder & Ember's own work exists, so this is the cheapest way to switch it on.

- **1.91:1 (1200 × 630), delivered at 2400 × 1260.** Text-free.
- The Alder at dusk in the hero's garden, with the cabin **centred** and everything important in the **middle 80%**, because platforms crop the edges differently.
- Must be the owner's own photograph, not stock.

## 6. Technical delivery

- **Format.** RAW files plus **graded full-quality JPEGs or 16-bit TIFFs in sRGB**. Minimum sizes are in the table in section 3; larger is better.
- **Choices.** At least **three candidates per slot**, not just one.
- **File names.** The asset id from section 3 plus `_01`, `_02`, for example `alder-exterior_02.jpg`.
- **Metadata.** Keep the camera data. Put the **model and configuration** in the caption of every file. No watermarks.
- **Colour.** Neutral white balance, true timber colour, no HDR look, no heavy vignette.

## 7. Rights and records

- **A written licence** covering website and marketing use, with no credit requirement (or the exact credit line), for each photographer.
- **A property release** from the owner of every garden or site shown, and a **location release** where the place is identifiable.
- **People releases** for anyone identifiable, including the installation team.
- **The manufacturer's permission** for construction pictures.
- **One record per image** (the site tracks this for every photograph):

| File | Model and configuration | Date and location | Photographer and licence | Product evidence or atmosphere | Real customer installation? | Releases held |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `alder-exterior_02.jpg` | The Alder, electric | | | Product evidence | No (showroom garden) | Property |

A showroom or yard garden counts as **product evidence** for the model shown, but it is **not** a customer installation and will not be labelled as one.

## 8. Installation stories (once real projects exist)

The "Our installations" section stays hidden until real, approved projects exist. For each one, shoot **6 to 10 images**, with the customer's written permission and approved wording:

1. The space before, in daylight.
2. The access route.
3. The prepared base.
4. Delivery and placement.
5. The finished exterior, in daylight and at dusk.
6. The interior.
7. One detail that shows care.
8. The finished garden from the house.

Never stage one to look like a customer's project.

## 9. Not on the site yet (optional)

A team portrait for About, the manufacturer's workshop, a commercial or hospitality setting, and Care & support. None is used today. Add them only with real subjects, releases and approved wording.

## 10. After delivery: what happens next

1. Each chosen photograph is cropped for its place on the site, and prepared in the file sizes that load quickly on phones and large screens.
2. Anything with text over a photograph is checked again for legibility.
3. A "Stock preview" label disappears from a slot only when the photograph that replaces it is marked approved, with its rights and a written description for screen readers.
4. The link-preview image switches on social previews.
5. **We need back, for every file, which model it shows**, so it can be used as proof of that model.
