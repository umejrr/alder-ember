What the price means

A starting point, not an installed total

Each starting price is for the cabin, including VAT. Delivery and installation scope, and anything that depends on your site, are set out in your itemised quotation.

Ground preparation, electrical connection, specialist lifting and non-standard access work are assessed separately, so we do not show a single all-in price before we have looked at your space.

We install in England and Wales. Scotland needs an individual postcode assessment. Northern Ireland, islands and other locations need separate arrangements.
