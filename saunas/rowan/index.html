Planning, delivery & installation

Checked before your order is confirmed

We check the space and access before confirming your installation.

View installation guidance →

  1. Find your model. Compare the three models by comfortable seating, size and starting price, or tell us about your space and we will help you shortlist.
  2. Check the space and access. Photographs and approximate measurements start the review. Straightforward sites may be assessed from photos or a video call; more complex ones may need a visit.
  3. Confirm the specification and quote. Your itemised quotation sets out the specification, what we will do, and what you or your contractors need to prepare.
  4. Arrange installation and handover. We confirm the schedule and site readiness before delivery, then complete installation checks and a handover with you.

Lead time
Approximately 8–12 weeks from confirmed specification and order acceptance, subject to production scheduling.

We install in England and Wales. Scotland needs an individual postcode assessment. Northern Ireland, islands and other locations need separate arrangements.

Questions about the Rowan

Good to know

After handover you have an accessible first point of contact for care questions. See care & support and the warranty page.

What does the starting price include?

The starting price is for the cabin as described on each model page, including VAT.

Delivery and installation scope, and anything that depends on your site, such as ground preparation, electrical connection or specialist lifting, are set out in your itemised quotation. It is not a confirmed installed total.

Who does what →

Which heating can I choose?

Electric heating suits customers who want straightforward controls and regular use. Selected wood-burning configurations suit people who are comfortable tending a fire and managing the maintenance and site considerations that come with it.

Equipment sizing, electrical supply, ventilation, flues and clearances follow the approved equipment specification. Warm-up times vary by model, heater, starting temperature and weather, so we do not publish a single figure.

Can I use a standard model for a business?

Commercial use needs a separate assessment of use intensity, occupancy, maintenance, cleaning, operating arrangements and equipment suitability. We would rather assess it with you than assume a standard model fits.
