Commercial projects

We take on selected projects for venues where guests or clients use the sauna. Each one is assessed individually, because a standard model does not suit every venue.

Intended project types

Who this is for

  • Boutique holiday accommodation
  • Premium cabins
  • Small hotels
  • Appointment-based wellness venues

How we assess

What we look at

We would rather assess a project with you than promise that a standard model will work.

How it will be used
Use intensity, occupancy and how often the sauna is used.
Maintenance and cleaning
Who looks after it, and what that routine involves day to day.
Operating arrangements
How guests or clients use it and how it is run.
Equipment suitability
Whether the heating and equipment suit the way it will be used.
Contractors and operators
Coordination with the people already involved in the venue.

The range as a starting point

Three models to start the conversation

The Rowan, Alder and Ember are a starting point. Commercial suitability requires separate assessment for each project, and bespoke changes need manufacturer confirmation before we can quote.
